Privacy Policy — Load And Run Games

Last updated: [insert date]

Load And Run Games (“we”, “our”, “us”) develops and publishes mobile games, including Hoverlane. This privacy policy explains how our games collect, use, and share information. We are committed to protecting your privacy and being transparent about the data our games use.

1. Information We Collect

Our games do not collect personally identifiable information such as your name, email address, phone number, or physical address.

However, because our games use third‑party services for ads, analytics, and leaderboards, the following types of data may be collected automatically:

A. Device Information

  • Device model
  • Operating system version
  • Country/region
  • Language
  • IP address (used temporarily for routing and fraud prevention)
  • Advertising identifiers (Android Advertising ID)

B. Usage Data

  • Game session length
  • In‑game events (e.g., score submissions)
  • Crash reports
  • Ad impressions and interactions

C. Leaderboard Data

If you use leaderboards, Unity Services may collect:

  • A unique player ID
  • Your submitted scores
  • Country/region

This data is not personally identifiable.

3. How We Use Data

Data collected through our games is used for:

  • Showing ads and rewarded ads
  • Measuring ad performance
  • Improving game stability and performance
  • Providing leaderboard functionality
  • Understanding gameplay trends
  • Preventing fraud and abuse

We do not sell any data.

4. Children’s Privacy

Our games are not directed at children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

5. Data Retention

Third‑party services retain data according to their own policies. We do not store any personal data on our own servers.

6. Your Choices

You may:

  • Reset your device’s advertising ID
  • Opt out of personalized ads through your device settings
  • Decline leaderboard participation by not submitting scores

7. Security

We rely on trusted third‑party providers who implement industry‑standard security measures. We do not store personal data ourselves.
